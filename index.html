Our Story

since 2009

The Qasr El Kababji restaurant chain was founded by Ahmed El Baz in 2009. The project began in Kafr El Zayat and expanded to become one of the most prominent grilled restaurants in Egypt. Following the success of the first branch in Kafr El Zayat, other branches were opened, most notably in Tanta and New Cairo.
