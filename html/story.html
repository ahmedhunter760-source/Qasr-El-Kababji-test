Who is Ahmed El-Baz?

Ahmed El-Baz is an Egyptian businessman and the founder of Qasr El-Kababji restaurants. About him: He started the project without any family experience in the restaurant industry. He relied on his passion for Egyptian cuisine and quality service to build a strong brand in the market. He began with a small restaurant and gradually expanded until it became Qasr El-Kababji.
